ABOUT

I'm a game and interactive media developer studying Arts and Entertainment Technologies at the University of Texas at Austin. I'm passionate about 3D modeling, texturing and coding, and I love technical and creative experimentation. I like building things people can pick up and play right away, from platformers to web apps and interactive stories.

I've released two Unity platformers on itch.io, Scale the Cove and A Cat's Journey Home, and made all of the code and art for both myself. I've also built a study-timer app with a Godot overlay, a weather-based outfit app, and a mobile-friendly Halloween story, and I'm working on an ASL learning tool.

I enjoy exploring different genres and pushing my creative and technical boundaries, and I playtest my projects with classmates and friends to make them better. I'm always eager to learn new skills, whether that's a new engine or a tricky programming problem, and to build with other people.
