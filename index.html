ABOUT

I'm an Arts and Entertainment Technologies student at UT Austin who likes making things people can actually play. So far that's two Unity platformers on itch.io, a study timer with a red panda in it, and a Halloween story built for your phone.

I use AI tools like Codex and Claude Code to build faster, but the ideas, design calls and testing are mine. I put projects in front of other people and change them based on what they say. On each project below, I'm upfront about what I hand-coded and what's still in progress.
